# 05 — Data & Platform Feasibility Map

*Research date: 2026-08-29. Scope: lexical/etymology data, on-device speech, geography/culture data & imagery, Android/Wear OS ambient surfaces, other watch platforms, SRS/storage.*

**How to read the markers.** `[verified 2026-08]` = I fetched the primary page this session and the number/term is quoted from it. `[unverified]` = from prior knowledge or a page I could not fetch (the search budget ran out midway, and several sites — Wikidata/Commons, Tatoeba, Leipzig, Garmin's SPA docs, Counterpoint — could not be fetched). Treat unverified license terms as *must-check-before-shipping*.

**Executive summary (the seven things that change your design):**

1. **The share-alike line is the spine of your data plan.** Wiktionary/wiktextract, EtymDB, ConceptNet, Dakshina, wordfreq-data and DBnary are CC BY-SA; CLICS³, WOLD, ASJP, Glottolog, IDS are CC BY; **Wikidata is CC0**. Commercial use is fine for all of these. Under CC BY-SA 4.0 a *derived database* that incorporates a substantial portion of Wiktionary is "Adapted Material" and must itself be CC BY-SA, but "simply collecting the work alongside independent works does not constitute adaptation" — so ship the word graph as a **separately-licensed CC BY-SA data pack** and keep app code under your own license. `[verified 2026-08: CC legal code]`
2. **Three sources you were counting on are non-commercial:** CogNet (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), **PanLex (its license page now says CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, not CC0)**, BabelNet (NC, research-only). Also MMS-TTS (CC BY-NC 4.0) and UNESCO WHC data (non-commercial). `[verified 2026-08]`
3. **Wiktionary coverage for your Indic targets is thin and very uneven:** Hindi ~35.7k entries, Telugu ~21.8k (mostly nouns), Urdu ~9.1k, Marathi ~5.1k, Kannada ~2.5k, **Konkani ~1.25k**. Hindi/Telugu are usable as a spine; Marathi/Kannada/Konkani need supplementary sources (IndoWordNet, Dakshina, AI4Bharat). `[verified 2026-08: kaikki.org]`
4. **Street View is incompatible with your ethos and your wallet:** ~US$6.6k–14k/month at 10k MAU × 20 rounds/day, imagery may not be cached (only `pano_ID`), and every round is a Google request from a key embedded in your APK. Bundle curated CC/PD photos (Commons, Mapillary CC BY-SA, Panoramax) instead. `[verified 2026-08: pricing + terms]`
5. **On-device speech is solvable for TTS (Piper voices exist for hi/mr/ur/te/ml/bn/ne/fa/ar; sherpa-onnx runs them on Android) but ASR for Marathi/Telugu/Kannada/Urdu/Konkani is not solvable offline with permissive models today** (Vosk only has Hindi + Gujarati + Indian English). `[verified 2026-08]`
6. **Phone ambient surfaces are good enough for "3-button micro-SRS":** notifications allow up to 3 actions + RemoteInput (API 24+); custom RemoteViews are template-wrapped from API 31; Android 16 "Live Updates" are explicitly *not* for learning prompts; exact alarms are effectively unavailable to you (USE_EXACT_ALARM is Play-restricted to alarm/calendar apps) — use inexact/WorkManager and accept ±10 min–1 h jitter, and expect OEM killers on Indian devices. `[verified 2026-08]`
7. **Wear OS moved under you:** current is **Wear OS 7 = Android 17 / API 37**, with **Wear Widgets (Glance-Wear + Remote Compose) replacing Tiles**, Live Updates and the new Ongoing Activity library "available since Wear OS 7". Tiles remain and *can* do an in-place 3-option quiz via `loadAction()`. Without Play services (F-Droid), the Data Layer is unavailable — your watch app must be standalone + direct network. `[verified 2026-08]`

**Share-alike scope — what CC BY-SA 4.0 actually says** `[verified 2026-08, legal code]`:
- *Adapted Material* = material "derived from or based upon the Licensed Material" that has been "translated, altered, arranged, transformed, or otherwise modified". Simply collecting the work alongside independent works is not adaptation.
- *Sui Generis Database Rights* (Section 4): you may "extract, reuse, reproduce, and Share all or a substantial portion of the database"; **if you include all or a substantial portion of the contents in a database in which you have database rights, that database (but not its individual contents) is Adapted Material** → ShareAlike applies to your word graph *as a database*.
- Attribution (TASL): creator, copyright notice, license notice, link to license, and "indicate if changes were made".
- Practical pattern used by Kiwix/Wikipedia readers: the app (Apache/GPL/whatever) reads a separate `wordgraph-<lang>.sqlite` that is itself distributed as CC BY-SA 4.0 with an ATTRIBUTION file listing Wiktionary + every merged source. Do **not** merge NC data (CogNet, PanLex, BabelNet) into that same pack if you charge money for the app.
- Wiktionary is dual CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL since the 2023 Wikimedia terms upgrade `[unverified]`; DBnary still labels 3.0 (3.0 permits redistribution under "later versions", so 4.0 is fine `[unverified]`).

**Design note.** Wiktextract already carries structured etymology (`etymology_templates` with `{inh, bor, der, cog}` template names, source language codes and terms) — for loanword/cognate/inheritance edges this is a richer, permissively-licensed and weekly-refreshed replacement for CogNet/EtymDB/etymwn. Use CLICS³ for colexification edges (thesaurus dimension), WOLD for borrowing *rates* per semantic field (your "loanword" difficulty), ASJP+Glottolog for family-distance scores. **Cost estimate (Street View Static, Essentials pricing):**

- Scenario A — 10,000 MAU, 30% DAU, 20 rounds/day → 60,000 requests/day ≈ **1.8M/month**: 10k free + 90k×$7 + 400k×$5.60 + 500k×$4.20 + 800k×$2.10 ≈ **~$6,650/month** (≈ $0.67 per MAU).
- Scenario B — every MAU plays 20 rounds/day → 6M/month ≈ **~$13,900/month**.
- Dynamic Street View roughly doubles both. `[tier arithmetic mine; base rates verified 2026-08]`
- Even ignoring cost: each round is a live Google request from a key shipped in the APK (key abuse; must restrict by package + SHA-1), violates your "no operator backend / on-device" ethos, and is impossible on F-Droid builds.

**Cheaper, ethos-compatible design:** curated bundled photo packs. E.g., 200 countries × 20 photos × ~50 KB (800 px WebP q≈70) ≈ **200 MB total**, delivered as per-region packs (~15–30 MB each) with an `ATTRIBUTION.csv` (author, license, source URL) — sources: Commons (PD/CC BY preferred), Mapillary CC BY-SA (logo + link-back), Panoramax. Add "hints" (script on signs, driving side, plates) from Wikidata. This is the only approach that also works for the watch.
